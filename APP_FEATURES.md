# 🍽️ MealPlan App - Complete Feature Guide

Welcome to the comprehensive feature guide for the MealPlan App - your all-in-one solution for meal planning, nutrition tracking, and culinary discovery!

## 📱 App Overview

MealPlan is a React Native mobile application designed to revolutionize how you plan, prepare, and enjoy your meals. With AI-powered meal creation, comprehensive nutrition tracking, and social features, it's the perfect companion for anyone looking to improve their eating habits and discover new recipes.

---

## 🚀 Core Features

### 🤖 AI-Powered Meal Creation
**Location:** AI Menu → Create Meal with AI

Transform your meal planning with our intelligent AI assistant that creates personalized recipes based on your preferences.

**Key Features:**
- **Natural Language Prompts** - Describe what you want to eat in plain English
- **Dietary Restrictions Support** - Automatically accommodates vegetarian, vegan, keto, gluten-free, and more
- **Allergy Management** - Ensures recipes avoid your specific allergens
- **Smart Ingredient Suggestions** - AI recommends ingredients based on your pantry and preferences
- **Automatic Nutrition Calculation** - Powered by Edamam API for accurate nutritional data
- **Custom Serving Adjustments** - Scale recipes up or down based on your needs

**How to Use:**
1. Navigate to the AI menu from the bottom navigation
2. Enter a description like "healthy chicken dinner for 4 people"
3. Add any dietary restrictions or allergies
4. Let the AI generate a complete recipe with ingredients and instructions
5. Review nutrition facts and save to your meal collection
